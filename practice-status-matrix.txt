================================================================
PRACTICE STATUS MATRIX
================================================================

----------------------------------------------------------------
RECORD TYPE 1: TRIAL
----------------------------------------------------------------
Status      | In activePracticeIds? | Counts to cap? | Can Focus?
------------|----------------------|----------------|------------
trial       | No                   | No             | YES
promoted    | No                   | -- (terminal)  | No
discarded   | No                   | -- (terminal)  | No
expired     | No                   | -- (terminal)  | No

----------------------------------------------------------------
RECORD TYPE 2: UPRACTICE
----------------------------------------------------------------
Status      | In activePracticeIds? | Counts to cap? | Can Focus?
------------|----------------------|----------------|------------
active      | YES                  | YES            | YES
paused      | No                   | No             | No
replaced    | No                   | -- (terminal)  | No

----------------------------------------------------------------
FOCUS OVERLAY (separate flag on PROFILE)
----------------------------------------------------------------
- todayFocusPracticeId is NOT a status on the practice record.
- It is a pointer on the user's PROFILE record.
- Valid targets: TRIAL(trial) or UPRACTICE(active) only.
- Only ONE practice can be focused at a time.
- Setting a new focus overwrites the previous one.

----------------------------------------------------------------
VALID LIVE STATES (what a practice can actually be right now)
----------------------------------------------------------------
State                  | Record   | Status  | Focused?
-----------------------|----------|---------|----------
Trying, not focused    | TRIAL    | trial   | No
Trying, focused        | TRIAL    | trial   | Yes
Active, not focused    | UPRACTICE| active  | No
Active, focused        | UPRACTICE| active  | Yes
Paused                 | UPRACTICE| paused  | Never

----------------------------------------------------------------
TERMINAL STATES (historical tombstones, never shown as live)
----------------------------------------------------------------
promoted  -- trial was kept, UPRACTICE record was created
discarded -- user said "not for me"
expired   -- 7-day trial window passed with no decision
replaced  -- practice was swapped out via Replace flow

----------------------------------------------------------------
SUBSCRIPTION CAPS
----------------------------------------------------------------
Plan  | Max active practices (UPRACTICE active in activePracticeIds)
------|--------------------------------------------------------------
FREE  | 1
PAID  | 10 (soft warning at 5+)

Note: Trials do NOT count toward caps.
      Paused practices do NOT count toward caps.
      Replace does not change cap count (1-for-1 swap).
      Promote and Resume both check caps and block if exceeded.

----------------------------------------------------------------
KEY MENTAL MODEL
----------------------------------------------------------------
Focus is an OVERLAY, not a state.
A practice is either live (trial or active) or not (terminal).
Separately, ONE live practice can be pinned as today's focus,
which controls what appears on the /today screen.
